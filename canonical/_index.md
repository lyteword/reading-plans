# Canonical Bible Reading Plan

The Canonical Bible Reading Plan is a straightforward, year-long schedule that guides readers through the entire Bible in the order of its books. From Genesis to Revelation, it provides a systematic approach to understanding the overarching narrative of Scripture.

## Overview

The Canonical Bible Reading Plan is designed for those who want to read the Bible sequentially. By following the books in their traditional order, readers can experience the progression of God's revelation to humanity.

Key features:

- Sequential reading: Follow the Bible from the first book to the last.
- Consistent pace: A steady daily reading load to ensure completion within a year.
- Overarching narrative: See how the story of redemption unfolds from Genesis through Revelation.

## Reading Plan

[January](./january.md) - Explore the books of Genesis, Exodus, and Leviticus.

[February](./february.md) - Dive into the books of Leviticus, Numbers, and Deuteronomy.

[March](./march.md) - Read through Deuteronomy, Joshua, Judges, Ruth, 1 Samuel, 2 Samuel, and 1 Kings.

[April](./april.md) - Study the books of 1 Kings, 2 Kings, 1 Chronicles, 2 Chronicles, Ezra, and Nehemiah.

[May](./may.md) - Explore the books of Nehemiah, Esther, Job, and Psalms.

[June](./june.md) - Follow the books of Psalms and Proverbs.

[July](./july.md) - Read the books of Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Song of Solomon, and Isaiah.

[August](./august.md) - Explore the books of Isaiah, Jeremiah, Lamentations, and Ezekiel.

[September](./september.md) - Study Ezekiel, Daniel, and the Minor Prophets from Hosea to Zephaniah.

[October](./october.md) - Read through Zechariah, Malachi, and the Gospels of Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John.

[November](./november.md) - Study the books of Acts, Romans, 1 Corinthians, 2 Corinthians, and Galatians.

[December](./december.md) - Explore the Epistles from Ephesians to Jude, and conclude with the book of Revelation.
