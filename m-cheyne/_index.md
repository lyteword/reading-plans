---
title: "Robert Murray M'Cheyne Bible Reading Plan"
---

The M'Cheyne Bible Reading Plan is a structured, year-long schedule designed in the 19th century by Robert Murray M'Cheyne, a Scottish pastor. Its goal is to guide readers through most of the Bible in one year—and the New Testament and Psalms twice.

## How it works

Each day includes four assigned readings:

- Two from the Old Testament
- One from the New Testament
- One from Psalms or Gospels

The plan is often arranged so that individuals or families read two sections privately and the other two are read in family or group settings.

By the end of the year, you’ll have read:

- The Old Testament once
- The New Testament and Psalms twice

## Benefits of the plan

- Broad, balanced exposure: You’re not just reading one section at a time—you move through different parts of the Bible daily. This helps you see connections across themes, history, and teachings.
- Built-in discipline: Having a daily structure removes guesswork and encourages consistency.
- Reinforcement through repetition: Reading the New Testament and Psalms twice helps key ideas and passages stick more deeply.
- Flexible format: Even though it’s structured, it’s adaptable—you can: read all four passages daily; split them across morning/evening; use it individually or with others

## Reading Plan

[January](./january.md) - Explore the books of Nehemiah, Esther, Genesis, Matthew, Ezra, and Acts.

[February](./february.md) - Dive into the latter parts of Genesis and Esther, alongside Exodus, Mark, 1 Corinthians, and Job.

[March](./march.md) - Read through the latter chapters of Exodus and the beginning of Leviticus, paired with John, Job, Proverbs, and several Pauline Epistles.

[April](./april.md) - Study the latter chapters of Leviticus and Numbers, alongside Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, and the Epistles from Colossians to Hebrews.

[May](./may.md) - Explore the end of Numbers and the start of Deuteronomy, paired with Psalms, Song of Solomon, Isaiah, and the General Epistles.

[June](./june.md) - Follow the completion of Deuteronomy and the start of Joshua, alongside Psalms, Isaiah, Revelation, and Matthew.

[July](./july.md) - Read the latter chapters of Psalms and Isaiah, paired with Joshua, Judges, Jeremiah, and Matthew.

[August](./august.md) - Explore the end of Jeremiah and Judges, paired with Ruth, Romans, Lamentations, and 1 Samuel.

[September](./september.md) - Study the latter part of 1 Samuel, 2 Samuel, and the start of 1 Kings, paired with 1 & 2 Corinthians, Galatians, Ezekiel, and Psalms.

[October](./october.md) - Read the completion of 1 Kings and the start of 2 Kings, alongside the Epistles from Ephesians to Timothy, Ezekiel, Daniel, Hosea, and Psalms.

[November](./november.md) - Explore the end of 2 Kings and 1 Chronicles, paired with various Epistles, Minor Prophets, Psalms, and the start of Luke.

[December](./december.md) - Study 1 & 2 Chronicles, the Minor Prophets from Micah to Malachi, and the Gospels of Luke and John, along with Revelation and the Epistles of John and Peter.
